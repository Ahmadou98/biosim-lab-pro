01 — ABOUT & ADVOCACY

A voice for stronger African health sovereignty.

I am a young Senegalese professional committed to the development of pharmaceutical sovereignty and local vaccine manufacturing in Africa.

My conviction is clear: Africa’s health autonomy depends not only on access to technology, but also on the ability to absorb it, operate it, improve it and pass on the knowledge.

Technology transfer

Building local capabilities requires more than equipment: it calls for knowledge transfer, process understanding and long-term partnerships.

Human capital
